A LITTLE CLARITY

Thoughtful answers.

A few things you might like to know before finding your next favourite piece.

How do I find the right size?

Begin with your bust or chest, waist, and hip measurements. Compare them with the measurements for the piece you love. Fit can vary between silhouettes; share your measurements through a Size / Fit enquiry for guidance.

Is every piece available in every size?

Availability changes by piece, colour, and size. Send an availability enquiry with the name of the piece and your preferred size. Our team can confirm the current selection.

What should I know about sale pieces?

The sale edit includes selected styles and sizes from earlier collections. Ask about availability and applicable purchase conditions before making a decision.

Can I collect a piece from the boutique?

Please enquire about the piece and your intended visit. Collection arrangements are confirmed individually, subject to availability.

How should I care for my clothes?

Always follow the care label inside the garment. Air pieces between wears, store knitwear folded, and keep delicate fabrics away from rough surfaces. Ask us if you need help interpreting a care label.

Can you help with alterations?

If a piece needs a small adjustment, describe the fit you are looking for in your enquiry. We can discuss suitable options and confirm whether an alteration service is available.

What is the returns policy?

Please request the current purchase, exchange, and return conditions before completing a purchase. Conditions may vary by item, and your applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.

Do I need an appointment?

Enquire about your preferred time before travelling. A styling appointment gives us time to prepare a thoughtful selection around your needs.
