Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Your privacy

This policy explains how information is handled when you browse SÉRAINE or prepare an enquiry.

Information you choose to share

The demonstration enquiry form asks for your name, email address, enquiry type, message, and optionally your phone number. The current static form does not transmit these details.

Enquiry preparation

The form validates entries in your browser and displays a confirmation message. It does not submit entries to a server. The displayed boutique contact details are fictional demo data.

Display preferences

Your light or dark display choice and reading direction are stored on your device. You can clear these preferences through your browser settings. These preferences are not used for advertising.

Maps and external services

The embedded map is provided by Google Maps. Loading or interacting with it may share your IP address and browser information with that service. Its own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting personal details. If you have shared information with the boutique, use the contact page to ask about access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.
