COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you visit.

Do I need an appointment?

No. You are welcome during opening hours. An appointment helps if you want dedicated fit or alteration advice.

What can be adjusted?

It depends on the garment. Common options include simple length, sleeve and proportion changes. Send the product name and we will explain what is possible.

Can I ask about a product before visiting?

Yes. Choose Product enquiry in the form and include the style name, size and any questions.
