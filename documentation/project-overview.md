# SÉRAINE — Project Overview

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
SÉRAINE is a contemporary, welcoming clothing boutique focused on curated everyday wear. The visual system uses warm ivory, soft off-white, charcoal, muted sage, and clay with clear Manrope typography. A restrained Cormorant italic appears only as an editorial accent. The shared folded-fabric symbol is used in the header, mobile drawer, footer, and favicon.

## Page Hierarchy & Architecture
The boutique includes 15 complete, responsive static HTML pages organized within `/pages`:

- **Home Page 1 (`index.html`)**: The primary product-led landing page, featuring a seasonal hero, category entry points, curated arrivals, a lookbook preview, practical styling notes, and shopping calls to action.
- **Home Page 2 (`home-2.html`)**: A wardrobe-discovery layout highlighting daily edits, knitwear, coats, and seasonal transition pieces.
- **About (`about.html`)**: Brand philosophy, atelier craftsmanship, material commitments, and timeless design principles.
- **Shop (`shop.html`)**: Complete collection catalogue with live client-side filtering by gender, occasion, and clothing type, real-time result count, and price sorting.
- **Product Details (`product-details.html`)**: Dedicated editorial presentation featuring the Solenne Gown and dynamic typographic collection studies for individual pieces.
- **New Arrivals (`new-arrivals.html`)**: A seasonal introduction followed by a complete product selection, quick-view dialogs, and size selection.
- **Sale (`sale.html`)**: A balanced six-piece sale grid displaying accurate original and promotional pricing.
- **Lookbook (`lookbook.html`)**: An image-led seasonal story showcasing silhouette, movement, and fabric texture.
- **Style Guide (`style-guide.html`)**: Editorial fashion journal indexing articles on tailoring, fabrics, proportions, and layering.
- **Article Details (`article-details.html`)**: Dynamic journal reader displaying selected editorial stories with pull quotes and reading times.
- **Contact (`contact.html`)**: Palermo store information, interactive Google Maps embed, operating hours, labelled enquiry form with helpful placeholders, and a six-question accordion.
- **FAQ (`faq.html`)**: Accessible accordion disclosures answering sizing, care, ordering, and boutique appointment questions.
- **Privacy Policy (`privacy.html`)**: Clear client data protection statements covering client-side preferences and enquiry generation.
- **Terms & Conditions (`terms.html`)**: Transparent boutique terms, garment care guidelines, and intellectual property notices.
- **404 (`404.html`)**: Styled error recovery page matching the brand aesthetic with navigation links back to the collection.
