QUICK ANSWERS

Before you get in touch

Useful information about fit, availability, enquiries and store visits.

How do I choose the right size?+

Use the size selector on a product to include your preferred size in an enquiry. We can then share available garment measurements to compare with something you already wear comfortably.

How can I check whether a product is available?+

Availability can change. Send a product enquiry with the piece name and preferred size, and the boutique can confirm the current selection.

Can I order or reserve an item online?+

This website prepares an enquiry rather than completing a purchase. Use the contact form to ask about a piece and the available purchase options.

What should I do about a return or exchange?+

Contact the boutique with your purchase details. The team can explain the options that apply to your item and purchase circumstances.

Can I visit the Palermo boutique?+

Yes. The address, opening hours and map are shown below. If you want a particular piece, contact us before travelling so availability can be checked.

How will I receive a response?+

The form prepares an email in your own mail application. After you send it, responses will be handled using the contact details you provide.
