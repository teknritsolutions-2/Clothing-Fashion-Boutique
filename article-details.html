THE PRACTICAL WARDROBE

Build a week of outfits from fewer pieces.

A useful framework for clothes that work together, fit comfortably and suit the weather.

Begin with repeatable shapes.

Notice the clothes you reach for when you need to get dressed quickly. Their waist, hem, shoulder and sleeve lines are your dependable starting point. Use those shapes as a guide when adding something new.

Pick two quiet base colours and one accent you enjoy. A shirt, knit, trouser and jacket in that small palette can make more combinations than four unrelated statement pieces.

PROPORTION

Check three lines.

Look at where the top ends, where the trouser sits and where a sleeve meets the wrist. Small changes in those three points often make the whole outfit feel more balanced.

LAYERS

Dress for change.

Wear the lightest comfortable layer nearest the body, then add warmth in pieces you can remove. Breathable fabrics help when the forecast and the room temperature disagree.
